Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

What is the difference between a social worker and a psychologist?

A social worker looks at your inner experience and the environment around you: relationships, work, family and living conditions. The work focuses on mobilizing strengths and creating change in daily life. A psychologist focuses more specifically on psychological functioning and may conduct assessments reserved to that profession.

Could virtual care suit my situation?

It offers comfort, access and flexibility. You will need a quiet, private place and a reliable internet connection. The introductory call helps us decide whether this format fits your needs.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed rule. Some people choose a few focused sessions; others prefer longer support. We review the process together regularly.

Which language will we use?

French, English or Spanish. We can also alternate between two languages if that helps you express yourself.
