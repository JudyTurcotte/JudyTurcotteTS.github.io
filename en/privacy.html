Privacy policy

Your information deserves a clear framework.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information is collected, why it is needed, how it is handled and what rights you have.

At a glance

  • Collection is limited to what is necessary.
  • No behavioural advertising.
  • Consent is required before form submission.
  • You may request access or correction.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Privacy Officer

Judy Turcotte, S.W., is the Privacy Officer for her private practice. For questions, requests or complaints, email judyturcotte.ts@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

When you contact the practice through this site, only the information you provide is collected: first name, last name, email, preferred language, general reason for contacting and your message. Please avoid including sensitive clinical details.

If services begin, information required for the psychosocial record, billing, receipts and clinical safety is collected directly within the professional setting.

3. Purposes

Information is used to respond, assess whether the service is appropriate, schedule sessions, maintain the professional record, bill and issue receipts. It is not sold or used for advertising.

4. Form and technical providers

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Form submissions are handled by Web3Forms as a processing provider. According to its policy, Web3Forms uses infrastructure including AWS, Cloudflare and Hetzner, and data may be processed in several regions, including outside Canada. Submissions are encrypted in transit, but a web form should never be used for an emergency or to share detailed clinical notes.

Once contact is established, clinical sessions and records are handled separately through the secure professional platform used by the practice.

5. Retention

Initial contact messages are kept only as long as necessary to handle and follow up on the request. Clinical records are retained for at least five years after the last professional service, as required by the regulation applicable to OTSTCFQ members, and are then securely destroyed when permitted.

6. Cookies and audience measurement

The site code does not install advertising or behavioural analytics tools. Cloudflare may use technical cookies or logs strictly necessary for infrastructure security and operation. When you follow a link to an external service, that service’s policy applies.

7. Your rights

You may request access to or correction of your information, withdraw consent where the law allows, or make a complaint. Some information must still be retained to meet legal and professional obligations.

Email judyturcotte.ts@gmail.com. Your identity may be verified before access is provided.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when practices or providers change. The update date at the top identifies the current version.

References: Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec and the professional record-keeping regulation.

Judy Turcotte, T.S.

Social worker and OTSTCFQ member. Virtual psychosocial support in French, English and Spanish.
